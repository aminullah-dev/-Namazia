# Privacy Policy — Namazia (اوقات نماز)

**Last updated: 26 July 2026**

Namazia is a prayer times application for Afghanistan, published by Kabul Signal.
This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

## Short version

Namazia does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There is no account,
no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. Everything you set in the app
stays on your device.

## Information we do not collect

We do not collect, and have no ability to collect, any of the following:

- Your name, email address, phone number, or any other identifying information
- Your device location
- Your device identifiers, advertising ID, or IP address for tracking purposes
- Your contacts, photos, files, microphone, or camera
- Usage analytics, crash reports, or behavioural data

Namazia has no user accounts and no sign-in. We have no server that stores information
about you.

## Information stored on your device

The following is saved locally on your device only, and is never transmitted to us or to
anyone else:

- The city you select from the app's list
- Your calculation method and madhab preference
- Which prayers you have enabled the azan for, and your reminder timing
- Vibration and dark mode preferences
- Your tasbih counter total
- A local cache of prayer times for the coming week, so the app works without an internet
  connection

Uninstalling the app removes all of this data. You can also clear it at any time through
your device settings, under Apps → Namazia → Storage → Clear data.

## Network requests

To calculate prayer times, the app requests them from the Al Adhan prayer times service at
`api.aladhan.com`. Each request contains only:

- The latitude and longitude of the city you selected from the app's fixed list
- The date
- Your chosen calculation method and madhab

These requests contain no identifier of you or your device. The coordinates are those of a
city, not of your actual position — the app never reads your device location. Requests are
sent over HTTPS.

Al Adhan operates independently of us and has its own privacy practices, available at
https://aladhan.com/privacy-policy

Results are cached on your device so that the app continues to work offline and to keep
network use to a minimum.

## Permissions the app requests, and why

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| Internet | To fetch prayer times for the city you chose |
| Notifications | To show the azan and reminder notifications |
| Exact alarms | To call the azan at the precise prayer time. An inexact alarm would sound at the wrong moment |
| Foreground service | To play the azan recitation and show a notification with a stop button |
| Vibration | For the optional vibration at azan time, and the tasbih counter feedback |
| Run at startup | To restore your prayer alarms after the device restarts, so the azan keeps working |
| Ignore battery optimisation (optional) | Only if you grant it. Some devices stop background alarms and the azan would not sound on time |

The app does **not** request location permission.

## Children

Namazia contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no user-generated content, and no
communication features. It collects nothing from anyone, including children.
